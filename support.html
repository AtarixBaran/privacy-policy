About Jewel Loop

Jewel Loop is a casual mobile puzzle game developed by Com Games Studio. Match, loop, and clear colorful jewels across hundreds of relaxing levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

The app crashes or won't load — what should I do?

Please try the following steps in order:

  1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Make sure you're running the latest version of Jewel Loop from the App Store.
  4. Make sure your iOS is up to date (Settings → General → Software Update).
  5. If the problem continues, email us at comgamesstudio@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

How do I restore my progress after reinstalling?

If you signed in with Game Center, your progress is restored automatically the next time you open the app on the same Apple ID. If you didn't sign in and your progress is missing, please contact us — we can help in many cases.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We love hearing from players. Send your bug report or idea to comgamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject line "Bug Report" or "Feature Request". Screenshots and short screen recordings are very helpful.

How do I disable personalized ads?

You can manage ad tracking from your device settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or revoke Jewel Loop's tracking permission. The app will still work normally — you'll just see non-personalized ads.

How do I request that my data be deleted?

To request deletion of any data associated with you, please email comgamesstudio@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (if any) with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process the request within a reasonable time as required by GDPR / CCPA.

Why does the app ask for certain permissions?

Jewel Loop only requests permissions necessary for gameplay (for example, notifications for daily rewards). You can change any permission at any time from Settings → Jewel Loop on your device.

I'm having a payment or purchase issue.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. For refunds or billing questions, please use Apple's official refund page at reportaproblem.apple.com. If the issue is with a feature you unlocked in the app, email us and we'll be happy to help.
